HARDWARE UNKNOWNS (what this checkpoint decides)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  V1 eased fades: every fade is 4 gradient segments (smoothstep). Expected: no visible line where a fade starts
     or ends (v1 showed bands at the HUD y~190/254, Home x~1120, series backdrop x~1280 / y~600).
     Wrong: faint steps / stripes inside a fade, or a seam between segments.
  V2 full-bleed poster layer: Home hero and details draw the poster cover-cropped (upper part) over the right of
     the screen, ~4x bilinear. Expected: a soft cinematic colour wash that dissolves into the page, no frame.
     Wrong: a visible rectangle, a recognisable stretched face, or the image competing with the title.
  V3 focus scale: posters (1.06) and Home cards (1.04) grow around their centre. Expected: crisp, no jump of
     the titles, no overlap with neighbours. Wrong: blurry text inside the poster, outline drawn offset, the
     poster drifting after many moves (it must come back exactly to its place).
  V4 rounded artwork: poster and card images now follow the 12 px corners. Wrong: square image corners poking
     out of the outline, or distorted images.
  V5 grid peek row: Movies / Series draw a third row that dissolves under the bottom fade. Wrong: the peek row
     drawn over the hints, popping in/out while scrolling, or images missing in it.
  V6 landscape cards: Continue Watching / Recently Watched show the upper part of the large poster (16:9).
     Expected: sharp, faces visible. Wrong: soft / pixelated (then only the grid poster arrived), or titles of
     the poster artwork cut in an ugly way on most cards.
  V7 grid poster upscale: catalog posters are drawn at 166 x 249 from the 140 x 210 image (1.19x). Expected:
     still sharp from the couch. Wrong: clearly soft compared with v1 -> tell me, it is a one-constant change.
