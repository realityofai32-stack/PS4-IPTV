IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG: note the step + photo. Log /data/PS4IPTV/logs/log.txt (GoldHEN FTP, port 2121):
"player" lines (tracks found and chosen, seeks, status), "xtream" lines (catalog sizes and times),
"images" lines. Credentials and stream URLs are redacted.

WHAT THE RESULTS MEAN
   Grid takes > 20 s the first time ..... 5.9 MB movie list download/parse on the PS4 (log "xtream movies").
   Subtitles never appear ............... libass font (log "subtitle font installed") or subtitle shader.
   Red "cannot be displayed" message ..... PS4 mpv build has no precompiled shader for subtitle drawing.
   Audio switch restarts the video ....... should not happen (runtime aid switch) - log "audio track".
   Seek is very slow ..................... software H.264 decoding from the previous keyframe.
   Resume starts from 0 .................. history.json not written (log "storage").
