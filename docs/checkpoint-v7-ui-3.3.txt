V7 IPTV - HARDWARE CHECKPOINT V7-UI-3.3: NEXT EPISODE FROM EVERY ENTRY POINT, 15 S COUNTDOWN
=============================================================================================
Branch feature/v7-ui-v3-polish (local only), one commit on top of v7-ui-3.2 (4110d7d).
Version 0.2.0, title IPTV00002 (unchanged).
Build ID and PKG SHA-256: build/checkpoint-v7-ui-3.3/BUILD.txt and SHA256SUMS.txt.
The V3.1 UI is unchanged.

What changed since 3.2
- Continue Watching and the Home hero's Resume now give the player the same episode context as Series Detail.
  They all use the same setup, and so does Downloads.
- Continue Watching in 3.2: when the series' details were not in memory (right after starting the app), the
  player got that one episode alone. So there was no countdown, and the end screen said "You finished the last
  episode".
- Now, when the details are missing, the player asks for them once (online). It completes its episode list when
  they arrive, usually within a second.
- The countdown starts 15 s before the end and shows 15 .. 1. The episode plays to its real end before the next
  one starts; nothing is cut from the end any more.

Install OVER the existing V7 IPTV (same title ID). Copy /data/PS4IPTV/logs/log.txt afterwards (FTP):
  [player] series details not in memory: requesting them once for the next episode    (Continue Watching, cold)
  [player] series details arrived: N episodes in the queue
  [player] next episode: <id> (S02E03, catalog)                  or "next episode: none (...)"
  [player] next episode countdown: 15.0 s left
  [player] next episode (end of file|play now|countdown): <id> -> <id>     exactly one per transition

THE MAIN TEST: CONTINUE WATCHING -> SERIES EPISODE -> FINAL 15 SECONDS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Close the app completely and start it again (the series' details are then NOT in memory).
    On Home, pick a series episode in Continue Watching that has another episode after it.
    It resumes at its saved position (as before).
 2. Seek to about 0:40 before the end (R2 / Right) and let it play.
    At 15 s left the panel appears bottom right: Next episode / S..E.. · title / Playing in 15...
    [SHOT with the HUD hidden]
 3. It counts 15 -> 14 -> ... -> 2 -> 1 and the bar shrinks; no "0" is shown.
 4. The episode plays to its very end (its last frames / credits are not cut). Then the next episode starts
    exactly once. Its title is in the HUD, and it starts from its saved position or the beginning.
 5. Home: Continue Watching now shows the new episode; the finished one is Watched in Series Detail.
 6. The hero's Resume button on Home (same episode type): steps 2-4 work the same.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS AND SEEKING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 7. Repeat 1-2. Press Cross early in the window (at ~12): the next episode starts at once.
 8. Repeat 1-2. Press Circle: the panel closes and the episode plays to its end. Then the normal "Up next" screen
    appears (X plays the next one, Circle leaves). Seek back 30 s before the end: no countdown again in this
    playback.
 9. Repeat 1-2. While the panel shows, press Left (10 s back, now > 16 s left): it disappears and Cross pauses
    again. Let it reach the last 15 s again: the countdown reappears.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OTHER CASES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Continue Watching with the last episode of a season (another season exists): the countdown shows the first
    episode of the next season, and it starts.
11. Continue Watching with the final episode of the final season: no panel; the "You finished the last episode"
    end screen.
12. Settings > Playback > Auto-play next episode OFF: no panel from Continue Watching or Series Detail. At the end
    the normal "Up next" screen appears. Turn it back ON.
13. Series Detail -> an episode: steps 2-4 work the same.
14. Recently Watched -> a series: opens Series Detail (as before) -> play an episode: steps 2-4 work the same.
15. Offline (if possible), Continue Watching with a downloaded episode:
    - the next episode is downloaded: countdown, then it plays from the disk;
    - the next one is not downloaded: no countdown, the normal end screen, no network attempt.
