PS4 IPTV - HARDWARE CHECKPOINT 1  (v0.1.0, title IPTV00002)
===========================================================

Goal: verify the production app foundation on the physical PS4 - new UI (text, input, redraw), Xtream sign-in
over libcurl, Live TV lists, and Live playback through the proven pPlay player.

This PKG contains NO credentials (the build fails if it would). You type your provider login on the PS4.
  Server URL: the part of your test stream URL before "/live/" (e.g. http://host:8080)
  Username / Password: the two path parts after "/live/" (or as given by your provider)

0. INSTALL  IV0001-IPTV00002_00-IPTV000020001000.pkg  ->  "PS4 IPTV" (new title, does not replace anything)

1. FIRST RUN                                                              PHOTO 1 (welcome screen)
   - "Welcome" screen, crisp text, controller hints at the bottom.
   - X "Add Xtream Profile" -> fill Name (optional), Server URL, Username, Password with the on-screen keyboard.
     Keyboard: X type, Square backspace, Triangle space, L2 shift, R2 done, Circle cancel.
     The password must show as dots.                                       PHOTO 2 (filled form)
   - "Test Connection" -> right panel: Connected, status, expiry date, connections.   PHOTO 3
   - "Save & Connect" -> steps Account / Live TV get check marks -> Home.
     (Movies and Series are not loaded in this build; their tiles show "Unavailable".)

2. HOME                                                                   PHOTO 4
   - Top right: your profile name with a green dot, and the clock: is it your LOCAL time? (yes/no)
   - Left/Right moves across the six tiles; Live TV shows the channel count.

3. LIVE TV
   - Categories on the left, channels in the middle, details on the right.            PHOTO 5
   - Turkish characters (ş ğ İ ı ç ö ü) in channel names: correct? (yes/no)
   - Hold Down in "All channels" for ~5 s: does scrolling stay smooth and speed up? (yes/no)
   - L1/R1 changes category, L2/R2 pages, Right/Left switch columns.
   - Square on a channel: star appears; "Favorites" category lists it.

4. PLAYBACK
   - X on a channel: Opening... then picture + sound.          RECORD video yes/no, audio yes/no
   - Triangle: stream information panel (expect ~1280x720, H.264, AAC 48000 Hz stereo).  PHOTO 6
   - X hides/shows the overlay; it also hides by itself after 5 s.
   - Down: next channel (switches after a short pause).
     NOTE: your provider refuses a new stream for ~30-60 s after the previous one closed. The app should show
     "Provider temporarily refused the stream (HTTP 403)." with an automatic retry countdown, then play.
     RECORD: did it recover by itself? (yes/no, roughly how long)
   - Circle: back to the channel list at the same position.

5. SEARCH
   - Home -> Search (or Triangle in Live TV): type e.g. "trt" -> results with a TV badge -> X plays.

6. PERSISTENCE
   - Home -> Circle -> Exit. Launch PS4 IPTV again: it signs in automatically and opens Home.
   - Your favorite is still marked.                                       RECORD yes/no

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   - Note the step and take a photo. The app log is /data/PS4IPTV/logs/log.txt (GoldHEN FTP, port 2121);
     passwords and stream URLs are redacted in it.
   - Screen flickers or freezes while idle (but reacts to buttons): create an empty file
     /data/PS4IPTV/force_redraw via FTP and relaunch - this switches to continuous redraw. Tell me if needed.

WHAT THE RESULTS MEAN
   Text garbled / missing characters ......... UTF-8 text renderer or font loading (log: "ui").
   Test Connection fails, PC works ............ libcurl/mbedTLS on PS4 (log: "http" / "xtream" lines with curl code).
   Lists load but scrolling is slow ........... list virtualization / redraw (log + photo).
   Playback fails, playback test app worked ... production player adapter (log: "player" / "mpv" lines).
   Clock wrong by whole hours ................. console time zone setting (log: "clock" line).
