HARDWARE UNKNOWNS (what this checkpoint decides)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  H1 UI clock: the app's timing now reads the kernel's real monotonic clock. In the log, ~10 s after start:
       [clock] after 10 s: ui clock 10.0x s, process time 1x.xx s, cpu clock y.yy s
     Expected: the ui clock advanced like the wall clock (compare the log's own timestamps); the cpu clock is far
     smaller. Wrong: "ui clock" much smaller than 10 s, or no such line -> hold-to-scroll is still broken.
  H2 Hold-to-scroll: each hold >= 1 s logs "[input] hold Down 10.0 s: N repeat step(s)". Expected for 10 s on a
     long list: N ~ 270-280 (Smooth scrolling on or off). Wrong: N < 50.
  H3 Real backdrops: the provider used in testing probably sends no backdrop_path (its get_vod_info has none,
     get_series_info has "[]"). The log says per detail page: "[detail] movie info: provider backdrop none|present".
     With "none" the poster fallback is what you see everywhere - that is expected, not a failure.
  H4 Poster fallback backdrop: the large poster (400 x 600) at its own ratio on the right, ~1.47x. Expected: dark,
     soft-edged art that melts into black on its left, no rectangle. Wrong: a visible poster edge or box, a
     stretched / squashed face, or the art competing with the title.
  H5 Black base: the page is #050608 and surfaces are neutral charcoal. On a TV in limited RGB range the darkest
     steps may merge; surfaces were kept >= 19 for that. Wrong: rows / panels invisible against the page, or
     any surface still looking blue.
