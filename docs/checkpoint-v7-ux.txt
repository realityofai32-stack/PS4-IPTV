    PgUp/PgDn, F, Del, S, O ...); press a DS4 button: glyphs again. No flicker while nothing is pressed. Unplug the
    keyboard while key caps are shown: DS4 glyphs return. Check Home, Live TV, Movies, Series, Details, Search,
    Settings, Local Media, Downloads, Favorites, Sources, the player HUD, Playback options, the end / retry panels
    and the Next Episode card. A shown key must do what its hint says on that screen:
      Home: S search, O settings, Del on a Continue Watching card removes it (F must do nothing there)
      Movies / Series grid: F favorite, Tab / Shift+Tab category, O sort, Enter details
      Series Detail episodes: D download, O episode options, Tab season
