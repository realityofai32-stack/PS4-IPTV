V7 IPTV - HARDWARE CHECKPOINT: IN-PLAYER LIVE TV NAVIGATION
==========================================================

Installs over the v0.4.1 compatibility checkpoint (same Title ID IPTV00002, APP_VER 00.41). Profiles, settings,
history and downloads are kept. Design: docs/v7-live-navigation.md.

Use a source with several categories. If you have them, also try an Xtream source whose provider has a guide, a
Stalker / MAG portal, and an M3U playlist.

A. CHANNEL LIST (play any channel from Live TV)
   A1  Left: the channel list opens on the left, on the category you came from, with the playing channel marked
       (accent dot) and selected. The video keeps playing on the right. PHOTO.
   A2  Up / Down / L2 / R2 move quickly; L1 / R1 step to the previous / next group.
   A3  Cross on another channel: it plays at once and the list closes. Up / Down now zap within that group.
   A4  Left, Cross on the playing channel: the list just closes (no reconnect).
   A5  Circle closes the list. Leave it alone: it closes by itself after about 12 s.

B. GROUP LIST
   B1  Left, Left: the group list opens to the left (Favorites, All channels, the categories, with counts). The
       channel column follows the highlighted group. PHOTO.
   B2  Cross (or Right) opens the highlighted group; Cross on a channel plays it.
   B3  Left, Left, move to another group, Circle: back to the channels of the group you had.
   B4  Open the player from Search: the first group is "Search results". From Favorites: Favorites.

C. GUIDE
   C1  Right: the guide opens on the right for the playing channel. PHOTO in each case:
       - Xtream with a provider guide: "Now" with time, minutes left, a progress bar, the description, then "Next"
         with the following programmes;
       - a channel / source without a guide (and every M3U channel): "No program information";
       - Stalker: the portal's guide, or "No program information".
   C2  Check that the times match your PS4 clock / time zone and the programmes match what is on air.
   C3  Up / Down with the guide open: it zaps, and the guide shows the new channel (its guide loads after zapping
       stops).
   C4  Left switches to the channel list. Circle / Right / Cross close the guide; it closes by itself after ~20 s.

D. STABILITY
   D1  Open and close the panels repeatedly while playing: no stutter, and the video never stops because of a
       panel.
   D2  Zap quickly with Up / Down (with and without the guide open): same behaviour as before.
   D3  Keyboard: Left / Right arrows, Enter and Esc do the same.

SEND: the photos, and /data/PS4IPTV/logs/log.txt. "get_short_epg" lines show each guide request (channel id,
number of programmes, size); no titles, addresses or credentials are logged.
