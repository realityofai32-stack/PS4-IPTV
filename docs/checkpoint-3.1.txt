HARDWARE UNKNOWNS (what these steps decide):
  U1 geometry: Aspect / Crop / Zoom / Position only change mpv's picture rectangle (Crop uses mpv's video
     margins, new in this build; same shader as every video so far). A black screen, a picture in the wrong
     place or a frozen picture after a change = tell me which control and value.
  U2 download speed: is the PS4 limited by its TCP receive window? Download diagnostics shows the socket
     receive buffer "system X KB, in use Y KB". Expected: Y = 1024 KB (or 512 / 256 KB if the PS4 refuses
     more) and a speed close to the PC's measured at the same time (step 19; the provider itself varies).
       - PS4 close to the PC                      -> the small window was the bottleneck; fixed
       - PS4 far below the PC with "in use 1024"  -> not the window: look at "Network receive" vs "Disk write"
       - "Disk write speed" below ~5 MB/s         -> the PS4 disk write path is the bottleneck
       - "in use" equals "system"                 -> the PS4 ignored the request (tell me both values)
       - PS4 and PC both slow                     -> the provider is slow at that moment

VIDEO GEOMETRY (M1, a 16:9 movie)
 1. Launch the app. Movies > M1 > Play. Select an audio track and a subtitle track (OPTIONS > Audio /
    Subtitles) you can recognise. Let it play for 30 s. Note the time.
 2. OPTIONS > Video. The rows: Aspect Ratio: Auto / Source, Crop / Fill: None, Zoom: 100%, Horizontal position:
    Centered, Vertical position: Centered, Reset Video Geometry, Set as default.                    PHOTO 1
    (Left / Right or X changes a value; it applies at once. Close the panel with Circle to look.)
 3. Aspect Ratio -> Auto / Source: the normal picture (whole picture, correct shape).
 4. Aspect Ratio -> 4:3: the picture becomes narrower with black bars left and right (squeezed), nothing is
    cut off.                                                                                         PHOTO 2
 5. Aspect Ratio -> 16:9: the normal full-screen picture again.
 6. Aspect Ratio -> 2.39:1 (and 2.35:1): the picture becomes lower with black bars above and below (stretched
    wide), nothing is cut off.                                                                       PHOTO 3
    Set Aspect Ratio back to Auto / Source.
 7. Crop / Fill -> None: unchanged picture.
 8. Crop / Fill -> Fill Screen: on a 16:9 movie no visible change (it already fills the screen); on a movie
    with its own black bars (M2) the screen is filled, the sides are cut, nothing is stretched.
    Also try Crop / Fill -> 2.39:1: a band of the picture's centre (bars above and below), and 4:3: the centre
    of the picture with bars left and right.                                                         PHOTO 4
    Set Crop / Fill back to None.
 9. Zoom -> 110%: the picture is a little larger (edges cut).
10. Zoom -> 125%: larger again. Optional: Horizontal position -> Left 100%: you now see the left part of the
    picture; Right 100%: the right part. Vertical position likewise.                                 PHOTO 5
11. Reset Video Geometry: toast "Video geometry reset"; the picture is normal again; the rows show Auto / Source,
    None, 100%, Centered.
12. During 3-11: playback NEVER restarted (no "Opening...", the time kept running from where it was).
13. The audio track chosen in step 1 is still the one playing (OPTIONS > Audio shows it ticked).
14. The subtitle chosen in step 1 is still shown (OPTIONS > Subtitles shows it ticked).
    Extra: pause (X), change Zoom, the video stays paused. Close the movie and open Live TV: the picture is normal
    (the panel changes were for that movie only). Settings > Default aspect ratio: Auto / Source, Default crop:
    None, Default zoom: 100% (unless you used "Set as default").
    If you have M2 (4:3 or cinemascope): Auto shows it with its natural bars; Fill Screen / a matching Crop
    removes the bars.

DOWNLOAD SPEED (DL = movie 100491, nothing playing)
15. Use DL (see the top). If DL was downloaded before, delete that download first (Downloads > Triangle >
    Delete download).
16. Movies > DL > Download. Do NOT open the player while it runs (playback pauses downloads).
17. Wait at least 60 seconds (Home or the Downloads screen).
18. Settings > Diagnostics > Download diagnostics. RECORD (photo is fine):                            PHOTO 6
      - Transferred in this session (MB)       - Session average (overall effective)
      - Network receive speed                  - Disk write speed
      - Receive callbacks (count, average size, per second)
      - Socket receive buffer (system X, in use Y)
      - Current speed and Peak speed
19. Compare with the PC control test AT THE SAME TIME: the provider's speed itself varies a lot (the PC measured
    anything from 0.4 to 2.7 MB/s on the same movie during one evening), and the account allows only ONE
    connection, so the PC must not download while the PS4 does:
      a) Home > Downloads > DL > Square (Paused).
      b) Wait ~45 s (the provider releases the connection), then on the PC:
           python -I scripts\measure-provider-download.py --id 100491 --seconds 60
         RECORD its "overall average".
      c) Wait ~45 s, then Square on DL again (resumes; this is also step 21).
    Earlier PC results for this movie: 2.2-2.7 MB/s when the provider was fast. The PS4 before this build:
    ~0.3-0.4 MB/s.
20. (done in 19a) Paused: the size stops growing.
21. (done in 19c) Resumes from the same size. Download diagnostics now: "resumed at ... MB", HTTP status 206,
    Range supported: Yes. After 60 s RECORD Session average and Network receive speed again.
22. With DL at 10-60 %, close the app (PS button > close application). Start it again.
23. Home > Downloads: DL continues from about the same percentage (never from 0; it may go back by up to ~32 MB
    or ~15 seconds of download).
24. Let one download complete (DL, or a short movie / an episode if DL takes too long).
25. Play the completed download (Downloads > Downloaded > X): it plays offline, audio / subtitles switch,
    Circle and play again resumes exactly.
