Root cause of the duplicated rows: recycled cells were bound only on the frame the scroll offset crossed a whole row,
and only the cells visible on that frame; gliding up, the top row could still be clipped on that frame and slide into
view a frame later still showing the row below it. Now a cell is bound whenever it is shown for another item.

 1. Movies and Series grids: scroll down several rows, press Up once - the top two rows always show different, correct
    items while gliding and after it; no duplicate row. Repeat many times (the bug depended on frame timing).
 2. Hold Up / Down, and tap Up / Down quickly in both directions: every visible poster / title is right at every
    moment; the focus ring and scroll position stay correct.
 3. Same in Favorites (grid), Live TV channel list, Settings, Downloads, Search results and Local Media lists (the
    same fix applies to every list); with Settings > Appearance > Smooth scrolling on and off.
