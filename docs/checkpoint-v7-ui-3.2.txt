V7 IPTV - HARDWARE CHECKPOINT V7-UI-3.2: NEXT EPISODE COUNTDOWN / AUTO-PLAY
============================================================================
Branch feature/v7-ui-v3-polish (local only), one commit on top of v7-ui-3.1 (f93852e). Version 0.2.0, title
IPTV00002 (unchanged). Build ID and PKG SHA-256: build/checkpoint-v7-ui-3.2/BUILD.txt and SHA256SUMS.txt.
The V3.1 UI is unchanged; this adds one playback feature (src/app/auto_next.h explains the rules).

Install OVER the existing V7 IPTV (same title ID). Settings > Playback > Auto-play next episode is now ON by
default, also for existing settings files (the old v0.2.0 option of the same name defaulted to off).

What to expect
- Only for series episodes that have a next episode. Never for movies, Live TV, M3U channels, or the last episode.
- The next episode comes from the provider's catalog order: the next one in the season, or the first episode
  of the next season.
- In the last 5 seconds a small panel appears bottom right, above the progress bar:
    Next episode
    S02E03 · <title>
    Playing in 5... (accent)
    a thin accent bar
    [X] Play now  [O] Cancel
- At the end, the next episode starts once.
- Only while the panel is shown: Cross = play now, Circle = cancel. Every other button works as always.

Please copy /data/PS4IPTV/logs/log.txt afterwards (FTP). It contains:
  [player] next episode: <id> (S02E03, catalog)          when an episode starts; or "next episode: none (...)"
  [player] next episode countdown: 5.0 s left             the panel appeared
  [player] next episode (countdown|play now|end of file): <id> -> <id>    the one transition
  [player] next episode countdown cancelled

TESTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Open a series episode that has another episode after it (Series Detail, mid-season). Seek close to the end
    (R2 / Right until about 0:20 left), then let it play.
 2. At <= 5 s left the panel appears bottom right. The HUD does not have to be visible. [SHOT with HUD hidden]
    Press Left/Right once so the HUD shows, and wait for the next countdown: no overlap with the bar, time,
    track chips or hints. [SHOT]
 3. The number goes 5 -> 4 -> 3 -> 2 -> 1, the bar shrinks, and no "0" is shown.
 4. At the end the next episode starts exactly once: no flash of the "Up next" end screen, no second start,
    title and episode in the HUD are the new ones. Its audio / subtitle choice and the video geometry follow the
    usual rules.
 5. Back in Series Detail: the finished episode shows Watched, and Continue Watching shows the new one.
 6. Repeat 1-2 and press Cross during the countdown: the next episode starts at once.
 7. Repeat 1-2 and press Circle: the panel disappears, the episode plays to its end and shows the normal end
    screen (no auto-next). After cancelling, Circle leaves the player as usual.
    Seek back 30 s and let it reach the end again: no countdown in this playback.
 8. Repeat 1-2 and press Left (seek back) while the panel is shown: it disappears at once; Cross pauses again.
    Let it reach the end again: the countdown comes back.
 9. The last episode of a season, when another season exists: the countdown shows the first episode of the next
    season (S03E01 or whatever the provider lists first), and it starts.
10. The final episode of the final season: no panel; the normal "You finished the last episode" end screen.
11. Settings > Playback > Auto-play next episode OFF: no panel; at the end the normal "Up next" screen (X plays
    next). Turn it back ON.
12. Subtitles on (Options > Subtitles), both subtitle positions (Bottom / Raised): the panel does not cover them
    badly. [SHOT]
13. A movie to its end: no panel, the normal end screen. Live TV and an M3U channel: nothing new.
14. Offline (if possible): a downloaded episode whose next episode is downloaded -> countdown and auto-play from
    the disk. If the next one is not downloaded: no countdown, normal end screen.
