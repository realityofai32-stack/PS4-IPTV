# V7 Player v0.5.0

**V7 Player — Media & streaming for jailbroken PS4**

V7 IPTV is now **V7 Player**: the product is no longer IPTV-only. v0.5.0 adds Live TV navigation inside the player,
programme guides, your own playlists, Jellyfin / Emby servers and basic Stremio add-on support, fixes USB drives in
Local Media, and hardens startup, saved data and privacy. It installs over every earlier version as an update and
keeps your sources, settings, favorites, history, resume positions, playlists and downloads.

> **V7 Player is a player only.** It provides no channels, subscriptions, movies, series, servers, add-ons,
> playlists, accounts or copyrighted content, and it is not affiliated with any provider. You add your own lawful
> sources and are responsible for having permission to access, play and download their content.

## What's new

### V7 Player

- New name, home-screen icon and start image; a loading screen in your chosen accent colour from the first frame
  the app draws (V7 Blue until you choose another), with no added startup delay.
- Title ID `IPTV00002`, the content ID and `/data/PS4IPTV/` are unchanged: v0.5.0 is an update, not a new app.

### Live TV

- **In the player**: ← opens the channel list of the current group, ← again the group list, → the programme guide;
  channels change without leaving the player.
- **Programme guide**: Xtream and Stalker short EPG; **XMLTV** guides named by M3U playlists (`url-tvg` /
  `x-tvg-url`), plain or gzip, saved compactly and refreshed in the background. Programmes are shown as the provider
  sends them; nothing is made up.
- **My Playlists**: create playlists from channels or stream addresses, import and export M3U. They appear as groups
  in Live TV, in your order, and can be hidden.

### New sources

- **Jellyfin / Emby**: sign in, Movies and Series libraries, details, direct play, resume positions and progress
  shared with the server.
- **Stremio add-ons (basic)**: one add-on per source, catalogs and details; streams with a direct HTTP(S) address
  play. Torrent, YouTube and external-link streams are not supported. No add-on is included or recommended.

### Local Media

- **USB drives are listed** (`/usb0` … `/usb7`): v0.4.0 looked in the wrong place and never found them.
- YouTube page addresses in playlists and protected Blu-ray discs are refused with a clear message.

### Reliability, privacy and security

- **Startup crash guard**: an optional startup step (keyboard library, playback backend, subtitle font) that crashed
  is skipped on the next starts instead of crashing every time; startup steps are recorded in
  `/data/PS4IPTV/logs/startup.txt` for diagnosis.
- **Saved data is never lost to one damaged file**: an unreadable profiles, settings, favorites, history or download
  list file is kept as `<file>.damaged`; sources come back from their side files; partial downloads are no longer
  deleted when the download list cannot be read.
- **Network failures** (no network, DNS, refused, reset, timeouts, redirects, damaged or oversized answers) end with a
  clear message on every source type; cancelling stops at once.
- **Log privacy**: no usernames, passwords, tokens, MAC addresses, credential-bearing addresses, add-on
  configurations or local file names and folders in the log.

## Install

1. Download `IV0001-IPTV00002_00-IPTV000020002000.pkg` from the release page and check it against
   `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
2. Install it with the package installer of your PS4 homebrew environment. It updates V7 IPTV / PS4 IPTV in place.
3. Launch **V7 Player** and add your own source, or open **Local Media**.

App data stays in `/data/PS4IPTV/`.

## Known limitations

- Jellyfin / Emby and Stremio have not been verified with real servers and add-ons on PS4 hardware.
- Stremio: direct HTTP(S) streams only; torrent and YouTube streams are not supported.
- YouTube page addresses cannot be played.
- Commercial encrypted Blu-ray (AACS / BD+) is not supported; data-disc playback is not verified on hardware.
- 13.52 startup crash: under investigation (see above).
- HTTPS streams use TLS 1.2: servers that only accept TLS 1.3 cannot be played.
- Stalker / MAG support is experimental and provider-dependent.
- M3U / M3U8 sources are Live TV only. No recording, catch-up or timeshift.
- Video is decoded in software; very high bitrates or 4K may not play smoothly.
- Thai, Indic scripts, emoji and some CJK characters show boxes, in the interface and in subtitles.
