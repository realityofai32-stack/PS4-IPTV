# V7 IPTV v0.4.0

A modern IPTV & media player for jailbroken PS4.

V7 IPTV v0.4.0 adds HTTPS streams, Stalker / MAG portals (experimental), Local Media and keyboard support, and
refines the Home, details and playback screens. It installs over v0.3.0 and v0.2.0 as an update and keeps your
sources, settings, favorites, history, resume positions and downloads.

> **V7 IPTV is a player only.** It provides no channels, movies, series, subscriptions, accounts, playlists,
> credentials or copyrighted content, and it is not affiliated with any IPTV provider. You provide your own lawful
> sources.

## What's new

### New sources and media

- **HTTPS streams**: HTTPS live channels, movies, episodes and HLS (including HTTPS segments and redirects).
  Certificates are always verified against the bundled CA list.
- **Stalker / MAG portals (experimental)**: add a portal with its address and the MAC address registered with your
  provider. Live TV, Movies and Series (when the portal offers them), search, favorites and Continue Watching.
  Compatibility depends on the provider's portal; downloads, EPG, catch-up and portals that require a box login are
  not supported.
- **Local Media**: browse internal storage and USB drives and play video and audio files with the full player
  (audio / subtitle tracks, subtitle style, aspect ratio, crop and zoom). Available from Home, the first screen and
  Sources, with no source required.
- **Keyboard support**: a USB or Bluetooth keyboard types into search and every text field and navigates the app.
  US and Turkish Q layouts. On-screen hints follow the device you used last.

### Playback

- **Next Episode card**: appears 45 seconds before the end of an episode (adjustable: 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds)
  with **Play now** and **Watch credits**; with Auto-play on, the next episode starts after a 7-second countdown. The
  card also appears when a file ends earlier than its reported length, and offers the next episode without starting
  it when Auto-play is off.
- **Continue Watching follows series**: once an episode's last 45 seconds are reached, the series' card moves on to
  the next episode.
- **Arabic, Persian and Hebrew subtitles**: a bundled subtitle font addresses the subtitle lag reported in #1.
- Smoother playback: library data is saved in the background instead of during playback.

### Interface

- **Home**: sharp, framed showcase poster (or the provider's backdrop), cleaner Recently Watched cards that can be
  removed, a "more" indicator on rails, and **L1 / R1** to move through the top navigation.
- **Movie and Series details**: plain dark pages with framed posters, provider episode images and the focused
  episode's synopsis.
- **Settings**: multi-value settings open a choice list; the D-pad only moves, ✕ changes the value.
- Fixed list and grid rows that could show the wrong item after fast scrolling.

### Reliability, privacy and security

- Sign-ins and catalog loads retry briefly after temporary network errors, with clearer messages for certificate
  and connection problems.
- Network requests are limited to HTTP and HTTPS; requests carrying credentials never follow a redirect from HTTPS
  to HTTP, and portal requests stay on their host.
- Stream addresses, MAC addresses, tokens and device identifiers are kept out of the log; the log size is capped.

## Install

1. Download `IV0001-IPTV00002_00-IPTV000020002000.pkg` from the release page.
2. Install it with the package installer of your PS4 homebrew environment.
3. Launch **V7 IPTV** and add your own source, or open **Local Media**.

App data stays in `/data/PS4IPTV/`.

## Known limitations

- HTTPS streams use TLS 1.2: servers that only accept TLS 1.3 cannot be played.
- Stalker / MAG support is experimental and provider-dependent.
- M3U / M3U8 sources are Live TV only.
- No EPG, recording or timeshift.
- Video is decoded in software; very high bitrates or 4K may not play smoothly.
- Thai, Indic scripts, emoji and some CJK characters show boxes, in the interface and in subtitles.
